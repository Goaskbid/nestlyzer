These are country or political-jurisdiction statistics, not municipal observations. No single global business score is assigned.

Economic structure

Manufacturing is part of industry. It is shown separately and is never added to industry in a composition chart. Value-added shares need not sum to 100 because of taxes, subsidies, revisions and source definitions.

Political institutions

V-Dem measures political institutions. It is not an economic-freedom index or an assessment of a particular company's legal security. The displayed SD describes the source model, not a forecast.

Business environment

The six captured B-READY values are explicitly reported by an official ministry. The full 101-economy data file is not included as a verified numerical import.

Weights are normalized to 100%. Employment is log-scaled. Concentration and price use fixed full-reference min/max values, so filtering does not change a state's score. An all-zero weight vector produces no ranking. Missing required observations never receive neutral values.

State screening

BLS: June 2026 payroll employment. EIA: July 2026 sector averages. This is a screening index, not a site recommendation. Available workers, premises and grid connection remain unverified.

Workforce scale and electricity price

Employment structure of state A

The energy estimate is consumption multiplied by the published average price. It excludes site-specific demand charges, taxes and contract terms not represented in the average. It is not a utility quotation or a complete operating-cost model.
These are source-backed rules for screening, not personalized tax advice. National, provincial and municipal components, tax bases, exemptions and minimum-tax rules differ. Rates are never added automatically or used as a global effective-tax ranking.

Institutional contacts

Public business contacts were not contacted. Phone and email availability varies. Recheck named roles before outreach; no form submission or email is sent by this app.

Funding and support routes

An allocation, tax incentive or programme ceiling does not establish eligibility. Closed deadlines are excluded from the default view.

Documented cluster references

This is a curated reference list, not a complete global cluster census. WIPO entries measure innovation output, not manufacturing capacity. The final-report narrative and older preview have different rankings; the source edition must remain attached to each entry.

Industry structure without false precision

The numerical employment charts use the original BLS industry groups. The GICS labels below are possible relationships, not an official quantitative crosswalk.

Source and API register

A documented API is not a successful API import. New values in this release were transcribed from readable published tables. Prior-source entries retain their original review state.

Measured release coverage

The original 129,508 active place records are preserved separately. They are not new municipal observations. Complete municipal coverage across every domain in all 45 countries has not been established.

Annual update contract

Rediscover releases and schema changes, preserve original periods, reconcile boundary changes, stage and validate new data, then approve deployment. Contact details and open calls need more frequent checks. A successful check without a new observation does not change the observation year.
