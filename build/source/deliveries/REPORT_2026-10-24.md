# Nestlyzer 2.10.1 collection and integration report

**24 October 2026. Scope: an incremental, partially completed data delivery.**

The package contains actual numeric files and integration components. It does not fill every one of the 177 requested indicators in every country, resolve all 555 geographies, provide worldwide local industry structure or complete the requested new-lease rent collection. Those gaps remain explicit rather than being replaced by national averages or invented zeros.

## 2. Requested KPI coverage

Every original definition is preserved in `config/kpi_framework_177.csv`. Every country-KPI control is preserved in `reports/kpi_country_coverage_7965.csv`: 177 indicators multiplied by 45 residential markets.

The existing-availability columns are declarations from the supplied task inventory, not new certifications. The fresh collection supplies some related context for **9 original KPI areas and 14 country-KPI pairs**. These are industry mix, establishment size, port throughput, vacancy, social housing, cycling, poverty, disposable income and housing overburden. This is far short of complete collection across the 177 indicators.

Several related measures cannot satisfy the exact original definition. Paris median equivalised disposable income is not unscaled household income. Eurostat population-based housing overburden is not a household-based share. French HLM occupancy is not automatically the same social-housing denominator requested elsewhere. These rows remain available as labelled context without falsely closing the requested KPI.

The package proposes **60 additional indicators** and selects 20, each linked to actual captured records. The selected indicators are household size, overcrowding, inability to heat the home, housing-payment arrears, car-free households, single-parent households, single-person households, housing-cost income share, owner occupancy, building age, outcommuting, self-employment, nonpermanent employment, elderly people living alone, recent residential moves, hotel rooms, port cranes, rooms per person, under-occupied housing and housing investment/GDP.

Coverage of those 20 varies substantially. Several household measures cover Paris only. Hotel-room data cover three French cities. Port cranes cover the report's top-25 container-port survey. European country values are selected figures from the publication, not a complete country-panel download. Rank is analyst prioritisation based on decision usefulness and available evidence, not a calculated market consensus or proof of exact adoption by a proprietary commercial benchmark.

## 3. US port data

The 400 source cells comprise 150 total-throughput values, 119 dry-bulk values, 65 loaded-container values, 25 crane totals, 25 terminal counts and 16 numeric on-dock-rail counts. Empty/dash rail entries were not converted to zero.

Throughput comes from the report's Appendix B and is for **2023**, despite the report being published in January 2026. Cargo uses million short tons; loaded containers use thousand loaded twenty-foot-equivalent units. Capacity relates to **2025**. Keep these periods and units visible. Loaded containers exclude empty containers, and short tons are not metric tonnes.

The broader port roster includes inland ports and territories. The surrogate `BTS_PORT_LABEL:` keys preserve publisher labels; they are not official USACE port codes. Port-authority, statistical-port and terminal labels do not always align. The package does not supply verified nearest-major-seaport distances, place-to-port links or a complete official port-code reconciliation.

Primary source: https://www.bts.gov/sites/bts.dot.gov/files/2026-01/BTS_Port-Performance-2026_Annual-Report_Final%2011326.pdf

The report's public-domain statement requires source attribution. Its crane and rail tables were visually checked against rendered PDF pages. Some appendix screenshot requests failed, so full page-by-page visual verification is not claimed. The tabular appendix was transcribed with units and ranks retained.

## 4. French municipal and industry data

The fresh Insee data cover **Paris 75056, Lyon 69123 and Marseille 13055**, using the dossiers dated 6 August 2026. The data include employee positions, employer establishments and establishment-size bands; active local units and births; hotel establishments and rooms by star category; selected facilities; and additional Paris household, housing, commuting and income context.

The exact requested industry import contract is `data/values.csv`, with 15 rows and all three municipality codes present in the supplied roster. That is a 3/3 code join for this small sample, not a full-France coverage claim. Sections A and F use employment shares. Sections J, K and L use active local-unit shares. Do not combine these denominators into one distribution. Multi-section aggregates and alternative establishment bases are separately recorded in `data/industry_group_values.csv` and in the dimensional canonical observations.

The original source does not independently expert-validate every local FLORES cell, and flags preserve that limitation. Boundary dates differ by dataset. Census principal and complementary extracts have different universes and should not be mixed to derive new shares. Facility counts refer to the whole municipality; they do not measure walking distance, driving time or facility density unless a separately documented calculation is added.

Hotel-room counts are 87,546 for Paris, 8,370 for Lyon and 7,660 for Marseille. These are stock/capacity measures, not tourist nights or occupancy.

Primary source URLs:

- https://www.insee.fr/fr/statistiques/2011101?geo=COM-75056
- https://www.insee.fr/fr/statistiques/2011101?geo=COM-69123
- https://www.insee.fr/fr/statistiques/2011101?geo=COM-13055

The Insee reuse notice dated 13 April 2026 states Open Licence 2.0 unless otherwise indicated, permitting commercial reuse with attribution, known update date and preservation of meaning: https://www.insee.fr/fr/information/2008466. The bundle does not reproduce Insee screens, branding or logos.

## 5. Eurostat housing context

The 83 captured figures come from the 2025 Housing in Europe publication's narrative. The observations retain the 2024 reference year, country or EU-group level, population/household denominator and published rounding. They are not represented as the latest full-precision API series.

The measures include housing tenure, household size, rooms per person, overcrowding, under-occupation, heating affordability, payment arrears, housing costs relative to income, construction value added and housing investment. National context must stay national when shown on a municipal page. Some series use population weights rather than dwelling or household weights.

Source: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/interactive-publications/housing-2025

Reuse policy reference: https://commission.europa.eu/legal-notice_en. Only numerical information is reproduced; third-party photographs and illustrations are excluded. Attribution and source-specific exceptions remain applicable.

## 6. Geography assessment

The mapping task's 127,707-place roster was used without altering native IDs. All 555 unresolved source areas have an outcome in `reports/geography_resolution_audit.csv`.

**102 source areas have addressable targets in that supplied roster. 453 remain unresolved.** The mapping file has 2,524 rows because some regions contain many place IDs and because each unresolved source receives an explicit `none` row. These rows do not create new local observations. A further 95 source geographies have 285 candidate-only rows in a separate file excluded from import.

The accepted code/name translations and recovered prior crosswalks are relative to the supplied roster. They are not independent boundary certification. Regional mappings can support labelled regional context, not municipal statistics.

Examples of controls applied:

| Case | Result and reason |
|---|---|
| Croatian Zagreb area | Zagreb County HR065 kept distinct from City of Zagreb HR050; the task example must not collapse them |
| Dutch Bergen variants | Bergen (L.) GM0893 distinguished from Bergen (NH.) GM0373 |
| German Laupheim | Unique supplied native municipality code 08426070 retained as a string |
| US Huntington, West Virginia | Earlier Census-place-to-native-place crosswalk retained with its evidence, not replaced by a name-only guess |
| UK post-reorganisation areas | Missing modern GSS-code matches remain unresolved where the roster uses older or split units |

Other unresolved categories include Czech districts, US counties, city-survey areas, functional clusters, Singapore HDB towns, Hong Kong district membership, New Zealand regions and territorial authorities, Taiwan regions and weather stations. Source stations need both distance and administrative consistency. A county seat is not a substitute for county membership. No silently inferred nearest-name fallback is used.

## 7. Business catalogue and household budget

The business catalogue reconstructs the **238 provider country/jurisdiction codes present in the retained master**. It is not the same as the 251-entry general jurisdiction registry or the 233 V5 country files. Some provider-specific entities are not sovereign states. Evidence completeness varies, including very thin country contexts.

An additive HTML fragment and JavaScript module are supplied for Claude to merge into the real `business.html`. The module handles the actual older V5 object layout, rejects conflicting duplicate IDs and treats approval as a strict boolean. It keeps the business selector separate from the residential universe. The demonstration is not a changed copy of the live site, and the current repository was not available for a production integration test.

The household module uses **150 recovered absolute-price rent inputs** across Norway, Sweden, Spain, Finland and the US. It rejects rent indices as cash prices, prevents known utility double counting, preserves per-dwelling versus per-square-metre distinctions, and requires explicit net household income and matching currency. It does not derive tax from gross earnings or reverse equivalisation. Missing expenses remain missing.

Norwegian annual per-square-metre values are divided by 12 exactly once. Existing-stock rents do not become new-lease quotations. Swedish annual-adjustment terminology does not imply new tenancy. The source's room definition is preserved. Wider-area rent requires explicit acceptance and remains labelled. All historical rent approvals are unchanged and false in this delivery, so the production path requires approval rather than bypassing it with research mode.

## 9. Validation and remaining uncertainty

The offline validation checks the exact canonical schemas, numeric and boolean parsing, record-ID uniqueness, alternate-representation agreement, original KPI-country control completeness, additional-KPI bindings, supplied-roster target existence, country-catalogue membership, rent arithmetic and publication/scoring preservation.

A temporary SQLite staging test imports 35,202 rows, then repeats the import with zero additions. This tests identifier idempotence, not semantic deduplication. The JavaScript suite has 25 passing tests covering budget safeguards and catalogue adapters. The current live application, browser rendering, current municipality boundaries and network refresh were not tested.

The code environment could not retrieve bulk API payloads. Web-readable official publications supplied the fresh captures. This operational limitation does not close the uncollected KPIs. The package's gap controls and false-completion flags deliberately record the remaining work.

## 10. Source refresh procedure

Use publisher bulk downloads or documented APIs where available, with bounded requests and saved original responses. Preserve observation period separately from retrieval and release dates. Compare source concepts, geographies, vintages and dimensions before matching. Store revisions as versioned source facts and record changed definitions. Re-run the offline validator and current-application regression tests before publishing.

Review Insee local tables on their annual release cycle, BTS throughput/capacity when a new annual report or official data vintage is released, and Eurostat underlying series when updated. EIA monthly prices and annual reliability have different update schedules. Rent releases also differ by source. The retained source register contains earlier refresh definitions; recheck them before executing. No recurring scraper, paid subscription, hosted collection job or deployment is enabled by this bundle.
