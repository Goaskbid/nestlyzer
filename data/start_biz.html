Questions companies ask

How does Nestlyzer rank places for a business site?

You pick the kind of site. Each one weights where similar companies already operate, freight access (motorway, rail, sea and inland ports), market reach, flat land, the labour pool and skills differently, and sets must-haves such as a motorway within 10 km or a major sea or inland port within 20 km. Every place in the country is checked and ranked; the weights and sources are on the Method page.

Which key figures are shown?

Each site type lists the figures a location planner compares: pay by occupation, employer labour cost, industrial electricity and gas prices, grid connection, outages, local business tax, land prices, the industry mix and more. Place pages show each one with its period, area and source.

Does a key figure change the ranking?

No. Key figures are shown with their source and never change the score, so a figure published in one country does not lift its places above another country's.

Is this investment, tax or legal advice?

No. Nestlyzer is a decision aid built on public statistics. Check permits, grid capacity, plots and incentives with the local authorities and utilities.
