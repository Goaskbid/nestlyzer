Schools, childcare and everyday facilities

This page fetches schools, kindergartens, playgrounds, doctors, pharmacies, supermarkets and hospitals from OpenStreetMap for the 45 Nestlyzer countries. It runs in your browser; nothing to install.

1. Press Start and leave this tab open. It takes about one to three hours.

2. You can close the tab at any time: progress is saved, and Start continues where it stopped.

3. When all countries are green, press Download and attach the file osm_facilities.csv.gz in the chat.

Ready.

    Data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. The page asks the public Overpass service one question at a time and waits between requests, as its rules ask.
